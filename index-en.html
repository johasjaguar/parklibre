What Can ParkLibre Do?

🗺️

Interactive Map

All public accessible parking spots on a clear map – currently for Berlin, Munich, and Dresden.

🔍

Address Search

Find parking spots near your destination. Just enter an address and go!

🧭

One-Tap Navigation

Navigate to the nearest available spot with one tap. No more tedious searching.

📍

Radius Filter

Perfect for finding parking spots in close or wider proximity.

🌐

Open Data

Uses official city APIs (WFS services) for current and reliable data.

🔒

Free Software

GPL-3.0 licensed. No tracking, no ads, 100% transparent.

About This Project

ParkLibre is an experiment exploring the challenges and opportunities of Vibe Coding – especially in the context of Free Software.

Created with AI models (MiniMax, Kimi 2.5, GLM-5) via OpenCode. Time investment: ~5-7 hours of prompting.

Disclaimer: This is my first app. I'm not a developer and created the app through Vibe Coding. Feedback is very welcome!
